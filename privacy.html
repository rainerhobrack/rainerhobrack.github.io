Privacy policy

Gmail Triage Dashboard · Last updated 7 November 2026

What it is

A private, single-user tool that runs on its owner's own computer and helps the owner sort their own personal Gmail inbox. It is not offered to anyone else and has no other users.

What it reads

With the owner's permission, it reads the owner's own Gmail messages through Google's Gmail API: sender, subject, a short preview and, when needed, the message text.

Where the data is kept

Only on the owner's own computer.

Who it is shared with

To sort each email into a category, its sender, subject, preview and sometimes its text are sent to TypeSafe AI, an AI classification service. Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

What it changes in Gmail

Only what the owner asks for, such as moving an email to Trash or reporting spam, and reporting new mail from senders the owner has already marked as spam.

Questions

Contact the owner through their GitHub profile.
